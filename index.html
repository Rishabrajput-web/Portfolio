About Me

I'm a final-year B.Tech Civil Engineering student at NIT Raipur, working across structural engineering and applied machine learning. I design buildings the traditional way, and I build the computational systems that model how people and infrastructure behave at city scale.

My research centers on urban travel-demand modeling — generating synthetic populations from Census data to simulate how a city actually moves. Alongside that, I build full-stack ML products, from clinical decision-support tools to satellite imagery pipelines.

On campus, I lead as Core Coordinator of the Civil Engineering Association and Overall Coordinator of NSS at NIT Raipur.

Urban Travel Demand Research

Fellowship-based research into how synthetic populations can model real urban mobility.

Hybrid VAE + IPU Population Synthesis for Indian Cities
INSA SUMMER RESEARCH FELLOWSHIP
IIT BOMBAY · SUPERVISED BY DR. ARCHAK MITTAL

Built and extended a population synthesis pipeline combining Variational Autoencoders with Iterative Proportional Updating to generate statistically realistic synthetic populations from Census 2011 microdata — a foundational input for four-stage travel demand models and agent-based transportation simulations. Work included debugging and extending the core VAE + IPF/IPU notebook, adding ward-level granularity, integrating a richer seed sample with mobility attributes, and authoring a full academic report with methodology flowcharts and validation charts.
